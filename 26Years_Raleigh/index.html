Turning 26? Here's what coverage actually costs.

When you turn 26 you age off a parent's health plan. That loss of coverage is a qualifying life event, which opens a Special Enrollment Period — normally 60 days — to choose your own plan without waiting for open enrollment. You can usually start the application before your coverage ends.

Your exact end date is set by your parent's plan, not by your birthday alone. Many plans run coverage through the last day of your birthday month; some end it on the birthday itself. It's worth confirming that date early, because it starts the clock.

The number that moves the price is your income

The sticker price of a plan doesn't change with income — the premium tax credit does. As your income rises, that credit shrinks, so what you actually pay each month climbs even though the plan is identical.
