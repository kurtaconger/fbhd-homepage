How we work

Fact-Based Healthcare Decisions is an independent, licensed North Carolina health insurance agency. We use data and software to compare every plan available to you, then meet with you to finish the choice and enroll. Our help is free; insurers pay our commissions, and they pay the same whichever plan you choose.

We recommend ACA-compliant plans only

On-Exchange plans

Available through the Marketplace at HealthCare.gov. We check whether you qualify for subsidies that lower your monthly premium and your costs when you get care.

Off-Exchange plans

Sold directly by insurers and available only through licensed health insurance agents. Some of the best options, such as select Blue Cross NC PPO plans, are only available this way.

What we don't recommend. Some agents sell other kinds of coverage, such as fixed indemnity plans and health care sharing ministries ("health share" plans). These are not ACA-compliant. They often leave out essential health benefits like prescription drugs, maternity care and mental health care, and they have no out-of-pocket maximum. That maximum is what caps your total costs for the year if you face a serious illness or accident.

How we are different

  • AI assistance — Harriette, the AI assistant in our Covanu ACA Selector, guides you through a short questionnaire about your goals and care needs, then selects the plans from the leading carriers in your area that best fit them.
  • Mobile-friendly data collection — Use your phone's camera to scan your medication labels and your list of preferred doctors. We check each one against the official HealthCare.gov drug and provider lists before using it to select your plans.
  • Total cost, not premium — Our tools search the drug lists of every insurer offering ACA plans in North Carolina to estimate what your medications will actually cost on each plan, not just whether they're covered.
  • Seamless enrollment — We guide you through the application so everything is set up correctly, including subsidy eligibility.
  • Year-round support — Questions about policy terms, claims or network issues down the road? We are here throughout the year.
  • Unbiased — We don't earn more by steering you toward expensive plans. Our sole focus is the smartest financial and coverage decision for your family or business.
