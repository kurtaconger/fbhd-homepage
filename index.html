Fact-Based Health Decisions

We are a Raleigh-based technology-enabled Healthcare Insurance agency dedicated to helping customers make informed decisions about health coverage using available data.

For ACA (Obamacare) open enrollment, November 1 – December 15, 2025, we can help you choose the right plan for yourself and your family based on your current situation and the insurance companies offering plans in your area.

What most agents do

  • Determine eligibility for government subsidies based on anticipated income and family size
  • Check whether your current providers are in-network
  • Verify if your current medications are covered
  • Help you choose the right coverage level (Bronze, Silver, Gold, or Platinum) based on expected healthcare usage next year
  • Assist you in selecting the plan which best fits your needs in terms of premium payments, deductibles, co-payments, coinsurance and maximum out-of-pocket limits.
