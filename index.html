Fact-Based Healthcare Decisions

Health insurance guidance for North Carolinians

Navigating health insurance options can be complex — whether you're starting a business, managing benefits for an existing small business, retiring early, or losing coverage for any reason. We provide data and advanced software tools to help you review and analyze every plan available, then meet with you to offer additional guidance and assist with enrollment.

Who We Help

Individuals & Families

Whether you're self-employed, retiring before Medicare kicks in, or losing coverage due to a job change or other life event, we'll help you find the right ACA-qualified plan. We can enroll you through the Marketplace (including evaluating potential subsidies) or work directly with insurers to access off-exchange options. Some top options — such as select Blue Cross NC PPO plans — are available exclusively through licensed agents in the RTP area.

Small Business Owners

We'll help you evaluate your full range of options, including individual plans, traditional Small Group coverage, ICHRA/QSEHRA, and MEWA arrangements (like Carolina Healthworks). We present the numbers clearly so you can choose what truly makes sense for your specific team and budget.
