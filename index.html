Fact-Based Healthcare Decisions

Health insurance guidance for North Carolinians

Navigating health insurance options can be complex — whether you're starting a business, retiring early, or losing coverage for any reason. We use data and advanced software tools to analyze every plan available to you, so you can make informed decisions based on data.

Who We Help

Individuals & Families

Whether you're self-employed, retiring before Medicare kicks in, or lost coverage due to a job change or other life event — we'll help you find the right ACA-qualified plan. We can enroll you on the Marketplace (with potential subsidies) or work directly with insurers to access off-exchange options. Some of the best plans, like Blue Cross NC's PPO options, are only available through licensed agents in RTP area.

Small Business Owners with Employees

We'll help you evaluate your options: individual plans, Small Group coverage, or ICHRA (where you reimburse employees for their own coverage). We'll show you the numbers so you can choose what actually makes sense for your specific situation.

What We Do

  • Mobile firendly Data Collection — We will provide you with a mobile app to collect your data and show you options
  • Analyze your options — We compare plans across all NC insurers using real cost data, not just premiums and make recommendations
  • Handle enrollment — We guide you through the application process and make sure everything is set up correctly.
  • Support you year-round — Have questions about claims, network issues, or life changes? We're here to help.

Why Work With Us

We're based in the Triangle and built our tools specifically for North Carolina. We don't earn more by steering you toward expensive plans — we just want to help you make the smartest choice for your business and your family.

As an independent, licensed agent, our services are free to you.
