How we work

  • ACA-compliant plans only — on-exchange through HealthCare.gov, and off-exchange plans sold only through licensed agents. No health shares or fixed-indemnity plans.
  • Total cost, not premium — our tools price your medications on every plan's drug list and estimate what a year actually costs.
  • Free, and unbiased — insurers pay our commission, and it's the same whichever plan you choose.
