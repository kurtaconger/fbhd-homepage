This fall's ACA Open Enrollment will be even more challenging than last year

North Carolina licensed Health Agents and Brokers are squeezed from two directions this Open Enrollment: not enough time to enroll new and existing clients, and changing customer preferences about how that work gets done.

Dramatically more plans. The 2027 federal rule ends the cap on how many plans each insurer can offer — analysts project 100+ options per shopper, especially in populous counties like Wake.
Relayed medication lists are usually wrong. Studies of patients relaying their own med list to someone else find discrepancies 94% of the time — the same telephone-game problem that happens when a client reads drug names to an Agent over the phone.
AI-empowered clients. ChatGPT is becoming the new “Dr. Google” for health insurance — clients arrive with their own research.
Phone-first clients. More than two-thirds already manage their health coverage from a phone — and many would rather choose their plan there too.

Your clients are already telling you what slows the process down

Can't I just use my phone to scan my medication names and doctors?
I'm too busy right now — can you just send me a link with my options so I can look on my phone?
Can I see all the data so I can analyze it in Excel or ask a chatbot?
I can do 90% of this on my own — I just want you as a second set of eyes to catch anything I'm missing.

Meeting clients where they are — instead of fighting these behaviors — is how Agents will survive and prosper this Open Enrollment.

What is ACA Plan Selector?

ACA Plan Selector is a web app that reduces the friction your customers experience, resulting in higher Agent throughput during Open Enrollment — because clients enter their own medications and providers directly, so the data you work from is accurate from the start.

1. New Customer Acquisition

After a conversation like “just send me my options so I can review on my phone,” you send the customer a link. The client scans or enters their own meds and providers, does their own analysis in the app, selects one or more plans they like, and the app sends that information back to you — already validated, with nothing lost in translation.

Fewer callbacks, fewer redo's

2. Reenrollments

For existing clients, send a link before Open Enrollment. The client reviews their current plan, confirms their meds and providers are still current, compares it to other available plans, and the app routes their selection back to you — ready for a quick finalization call.

Faster renewals, fewer surprises

The focus remains the Agent

Health insurance is a complex, high-stakes decision — which is why human Agents keep gaining ground. Per CMS, Agents and Brokers assisted roughly 3 out of 4 HealthCare.gov enrollments in recent Open Enrollments (78% in 2025, 76% in 2026), up from about half a decade ago.

We see ACA Plan Selector as a tool that makes it easier for clients to do the initial analysis themselves — then come to you to get their tough questions answered and finalize with confidence.
